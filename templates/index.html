About SDG 6

Sustainable Development Goal 6 aims to ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all.

Access to clean water and sanitation is fundamental to human health, environmental sustainability and economic development.

  • Better sanitation awareness.

  • Improved waste management practices.
